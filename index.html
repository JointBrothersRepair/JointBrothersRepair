About Us

Joint Brothers Trucks and Trailer Repair Inc is a family-owned heavy-duty repair shop located in Mississauga, Ontario. Founded by brothers Dilpreet and Jaskaran Singh, we built this business on a commitment to honest, high-quality workmanship.

From single owner-operators to large fleets, we treat every vehicle like our own. Our technicians are experienced with all major makes of diesel trucks and trailers, and we back every job with our dedication to getting you back on the road fast.
